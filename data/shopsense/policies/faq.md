# ShopSense Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I return an item without the original box?

Only if the item itself is undamaged and all accessories are included -- see Return Policy section RP-3.1. Missing original packaging may trigger a partial refund deduction under Refund Policy section RF-1.2.

## How long does a refund take after my return is picked up?

Once the item passes warehouse quality check (QC must complete within 48 hours of receipt, SLA code SLA-48), refund timing depends on your refund method -- see the table in Refund Policy section RF-2. Card refunds typically take 5-7 business days after QC completes.

## My order says delivered but I never received it.

First check with neighbors or building security, since carrier "delivered" scans are sometimes early by a few hours. If you still can't locate it after 24 hours, contact support. This is treated as a lost-shipment inquiry and may lead to escalation code ESC-LOST (Shipping Policy section SP-4.1) once the carrier confirms no valid delivery.

## Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?

No. Once an order has a shipment record with a tracking number (carrier: BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, Ecom Express, or India Post), it cannot be cancelled -- only returned once delivered, following the Return Policy. Orders still in processing can be cancelled free; see Cancellation Policy CN-1.1.

## What is ShopSense Plus and does it change return fees?

ShopSense Plus members do not pay the INR 99 pickup fee referenced in Return Policy section RP-4 for standard (RP-1.1 or RP-1.2) return reasons. Defective or wrong-item returns (RP-1.3) are always free regardless of membership.

## I was charged twice for the same order.

This is a duplicate charge and is refunded in full within 3 business days once finance confirms it -- see Refund Policy section RF-4.2. It does not require a manager or executive approval tier.

## My product arrived and it's a safety hazard (sparking, smells of burning, etc.).

Stop using it immediately and contact support right away. This is routed under escalation code ESC-SAFETY with a 1-hour first-response SLA, per the Escalation SOP.
