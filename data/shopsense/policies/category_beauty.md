# ShopSense Category Addendum: Beauty and Personal Care

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to the **beauty** category (serums, sunscreen, hair dryers, lip balm). Where this addendum conflicts with the general Return Policy (return_policy.md), **this addendum wins** for beauty and personal care.

## CA-BT-1 Return Window

### CA-BT-1.1 Standard Beauty Window

Beauty items can be returned within **10 days** of delivery, much shorter than the general 30 days in RP-1.1.

### CA-BT-1.2 No Festive Extension

The festive season window in RP-1.2 does **not** apply to beauty and personal care items. The window stays at 10 days all year.

## CA-BT-2 Hygiene Seal

### CA-BT-2.1 Sealed Items Only

Only items with an **unbroken hygiene seal** are returnable for change of mind. Once a serum, sunscreen or lip balm has been opened, it cannot be returned unless defective (RP-1.3).

### CA-BT-2.2 Skin Reactions

A reaction to a product is not a defect under RP-1.3. Support may offer a refund as a goodwill gesture of up to INR 500 under refund_authorisation_matrix.md RA-4, with a photo of the reaction; anything above that goes to a manager.

## CA-BT-3 Beauty Appliances

### CA-BT-3.1 Hair Dryers and Similar

Electrical beauty appliances (hair dryers, straighteners, trimmers) follow the 10-day beauty return window, but are unopened-or-defective only. They carry the 6-month warranty in warranty_policy.md (WP-3).

## CA-BT-4 Related Policies

See Return Policy RP-2.1 for the general exclusion of opened personal care items.
