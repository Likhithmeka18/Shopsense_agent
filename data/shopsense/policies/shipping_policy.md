# ShopSense Shipping Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Covers delivery timelines, carriers, and what happens when a shipment is delayed or lost.

## SP-1 Standard Delivery Timelines

### SP-1.1 Metro Areas

Orders to Tier-1 metro areas (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune) are delivered within **2-4 business days** of dispatch.

### SP-1.2 Non-Metro Areas

Orders to other serviceable pin codes are delivered within **5-8 business days** of dispatch.

### SP-1.3 Remote / Hard-to-Serve Pin Codes

A small set of remote pin codes (maintained separately) may take up to **12 business days**; these are flagged at checkout with a notice.

## SP-2 Carriers

ShopSense ships via five partner carriers depending on destination and package weight: BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, Ecom Express, and India Post. The carrier and tracking number are shown in the order's shipment record once dispatched.

## SP-3 Delayed Shipments

### SP-3.1 What Counts as Delayed

A shipment is considered delayed if it has not moved (no new tracking scan) for more than **3 calendar days**, or if it has not been delivered by the estimated delivery date shown at checkout plus a 2-day grace period.

### SP-3.2 Delay Compensation

For delayed shipments confirmed under SP-3.1, the customer is offered, at their choice: (a) a shipping fee waiver on this order, or (b) a ShopSense credit of INR 100 for the inconvenience. This does not apply if the delay is caused by an incorrect address supplied by the customer.

## SP-4 Lost Shipments

### SP-4.1 Declaring a Shipment Lost

A shipment is declared lost -- escalation code **ESC-LOST** -- if there has been no tracking movement for **10 calendar days**. Once declared lost, the order qualifies for a full refund under Refund Policy section RF-4.1, or a free reshipment if stock is available, at the customer's choice.

## SP-5 Related Policies

See the Refund Policy (document: refund_policy.md) section RF-4.1 for refunds on orders that never arrived, and the Escalation SOP (document: escalation_sop.md) for how lost-shipment cases (ESC-LOST) are routed to a human agent.
