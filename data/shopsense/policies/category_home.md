# ShopSense Category Addendum: Home and Kitchen

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to the **home** category (cookware, lamps, pillows, mugs, storage boxes). For most home items the general Return Policy applies unchanged; this addendum states the exceptions.

## CA-HM-1 Return Window

### CA-HM-1.1 Standard Home Window

Home and kitchen items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery, the same as the general window in RP-1.1.

### CA-HM-1.2 Festive Season Home Window

For orders placed between 1 October and 31 December the home window extends to **45 days** from delivery, as in RP-1.2.

## CA-HM-2 Breakables and Bulky Items

### CA-HM-2.1 Breakage in Transit

Ceramic, glass and other breakable items that arrive broken must be reported within **7 days** of delivery, not the general 60 days in RP-1.3, with photos of the item and the outer box.

### CA-HM-2.2 Bulky Items

Items heavier than 10 kg or longer than 1 metre carry a pickup fee of **INR 299** for change-of-mind returns instead of the flat INR 99 in RP-4. The fee is waived for defective or wrong items.

## CA-HM-3 Condition Rules

### CA-HM-3.1 Pillows and Bedding

Pillows and bedding are returnable only if unused and still in their sealed vacuum pack. An opened pack is treated as used.

### CA-HM-3.2 Cookware

Cookware must be unused and uncleaned of its protective film. A used pan is not returnable unless defective.

## CA-HM-4 Related Policies

Home electrical items such as lamps are covered by the 6-month warranty in warranty_policy.md (WP-2).
