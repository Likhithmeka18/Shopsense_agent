# ShopSense Category Addendum: Apparel and Footwear

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to the **apparel** category (t-shirts, jackets, shirts, sweaters, yoga pants, running shoes). Where this addendum conflicts with the general Return Policy (return_policy.md), **this addendum wins** for apparel.

## CA-AP-1 Return Window

### CA-AP-1.1 Standard Apparel Window

Apparel can be returned within **45 days** of delivery, longer than the general 30 days in RP-1.1, because fit cannot be judged until the garment is tried on.

### CA-AP-1.2 Festive Season Apparel Window

For orders placed between 1 October and 31 December the apparel window extends to **60 days** from delivery.

## CA-AP-2 Condition Rules

### CA-AP-2.1 Tags and Wear

All tags must be attached and the item must be unwashed and unworn, except for a try-on. Visible wear, perfume or deodorant marks, or removed tags can reduce the refund by up to 20% (Refund Policy RF-1.2).

### CA-AP-2.2 Innerwear and Swimwear

Innerwear, socks and swimwear cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken, unless defective (Return Policy RP-1.3).

## CA-AP-3 Size Exchanges

### CA-AP-3.1 Free Size Exchange

The first size exchange on any apparel order is free and has no pickup fee. A second exchange on the same order carries the standard INR 99 pickup fee from Return Policy RP-4.

### CA-AP-3.2 Footwear

Running shoes must be returned with the original shoebox and without outdoor dirt on the soles. Shoes worn outdoors are treated as used under CA-AP-2.1.

## CA-AP-4 Related Policies

Apparel carries no manufacturer warranty (see warranty_policy.md WP-4). Refund approval limits are in refund_authorisation_matrix.md.
