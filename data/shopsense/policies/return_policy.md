# ShopSense Return Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to all orders placed through the ShopSense storefront and mobile app.

## RP-1 Return Window

### RP-1.1 Standard Window

Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund or exchange, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging, and accompanied by the order confirmation or invoice.

### RP-1.4 Category Addenda Take Precedence

The 30-day window above is the general default. Each product category has its own addendum (category_electronics.md, category_apparel.md, category_groceries.md, category_home.md, category_beauty.md) with its own window. Where an addendum differs from this policy, **the addendum wins** for that category. Check the item's category before quoting a window.

### RP-1.2 Extended Window for Festive Season

Orders placed between 1 October and 31 December qualify for an extended **45-day** return window, to account for gifting.

### RP-1.3 Defective or Wrong Item

If an item arrives defective, damaged, or different from what was ordered, the customer has **60 days** from delivery to report it, and no restocking fee applies regardless of condition.

## RP-2 Non-Returnable Items

### RP-2.1 Category Exclusions

The following categories cannot be returned once delivered, except where RP-1.3 (defective/wrong item) applies:

- Perishable goods (groceries, fresh flowers)
- Personal care items with a broken hygiene seal
- Gift cards and store credit
- Made-to-order or customized products

### RP-2.2 Final Sale Items

Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout are not eligible for return or exchange under any circumstances.

## RP-3 Return Condition Requirements

### RP-3.1 Packaging

Items must be returned in their original packaging with all tags, manuals, and accessories included. Missing original packaging may trigger a partial refund deduction (see Refund Policy, RF-1.2).

### RP-3.2 Electronics With an Opened Anti-Tamper Seal

Electronics with an opened anti-tamper seal can only be returned if defective (see RP-1.3); buyer's-remorse returns on opened electronics are not accepted.

## RP-4 How to Initiate a Return

1. Open the order in "My Orders" and select "Return or Replace".
2. Choose a reason code from the dropdown.
3. Print the prepaid return label. It is free for RP-1.3 (defective/wrong item) cases; a flat INR 99 pickup fee applies for RP-1.1 and RP-1.2 reasons unless the customer has ShopSense Plus membership.
4. Hand the package to the courier within 7 days of label generation, or it expires and must be re-requested.
