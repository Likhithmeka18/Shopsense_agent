# ShopSense Category Addendum: Groceries and Perishables

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to the **groceries** category (rice, honey, tea, almonds, cold pressed oil). Where this addendum conflicts with the general Return Policy (return_policy.md), **this addendum wins** for groceries.

## CA-GR-1 Returns

### CA-GR-1.1 Not Returnable

Grocery items are **not returnable** for change of mind, taste or preference. The general 30-day window (RP-1.1), the festive window (RP-1.2) and the 60-day defective-item window (RP-1.3) do **not** apply to groceries.

### CA-GR-1.2 Problem Reports Within 48 Hours

A grocery item that arrives damaged, spoiled, expired or different from the order must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery, with a photo of the item and its batch or expiry label. Reports after 48 hours are refused.

## CA-GR-2 Resolution

### CA-GR-2.1 Refund or Replacement

For a valid report under CA-GR-1.2 the customer chooses a refund or a replacement. No pickup is arranged for groceries; the customer is asked to discard the item. No pickup fee applies.

### CA-GR-2.2 Shelf Life

ShopSense ships groceries with at least 60% of their shelf life remaining at delivery. An item delivered below that is treated as spoiled under CA-GR-1.2 even if the date has not passed.

## CA-GR-3 Related Policies

Refund timing follows Refund Policy RF-2. Refunds under INR 2,000, which covers every single grocery item, are auto-approved (refund_authorisation_matrix.md).
