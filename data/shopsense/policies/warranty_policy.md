# ShopSense Warranty Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. A warranty covers manufacturing faults after the return window has ended. It is separate from returns: a return is a change-of-mind or a faulty-on-arrival remedy; a warranty claim is a fault that appears later during normal use.

## WP-1 Electronics

### WP-1.1 Coverage Period

Electronics carry a **12-month** warranty from the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use.

### WP-1.2 Claim Route

During the first **7 days** after delivery, a faulty electronic item is handled as dead-on-arrival under CA-EL (category_electronics.md). From day 8 to month 12, the claim is a warranty claim: the customer sends the item for inspection and ShopSense repairs or replaces it. A cash refund is only given if repair and replacement are both impossible.

## WP-2 Home Electrical Items

Home electrical items (for example kettles, mixers, heaters) carry a **6-month** warranty from delivery. Non-electrical home items such as cookware, storage and decor are not covered by a warranty; faults are handled within the return window in CA-HM (category_home.md).

## WP-3 Beauty Appliances

Electrical beauty appliances (hair dryers, straighteners, trimmers) carry a **6-month** warranty from delivery. Beauty consumables such as serums, sunscreen and lip balm are not covered by any warranty.

## WP-4 Apparel and Groceries

Apparel has **no warranty**. Seam failure or defects discovered at delivery are handled as defective items under RP-1.3 (60 days to report). Groceries have no warranty; problems must be reported within 48 hours under CA-GR.

## WP-5 What a Warranty Does Not Cover

- Physical damage, liquid damage, burns or misuse after delivery.
- Repairs or modification by anyone other than an authorised service centre.
- Consumable parts such as batteries, filters and brushes, once used.
- Items bought outside ShopSense.

### WP-5.1 Proof of Purchase

The order confirmation or invoice number is the proof of purchase. A claim without a matching ShopSense order cannot be accepted.

## WP-6 Related Policies

See Return Policy (return_policy.md) RP-1.3 for faulty-on-arrival items and the category addenda for the return window of each product type. A safety hazard is never a warranty claim; it is escalated immediately under ESC-SAFETY in the Escalation SOP.
