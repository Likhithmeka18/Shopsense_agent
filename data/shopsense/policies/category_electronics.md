# ShopSense Category Addendum: Electronics

Effective 2026-01-01. Applies to products in the **electronics** category (earbuds, speakers, power banks, chargers, smartwatches, laptop sleeves, keyboards). Where this addendum conflicts with the general Return Policy (return_policy.md), **this addendum wins** for electronics.

## CA-EL-1 Return Window

### CA-EL-1.1 Standard Electronics Window

Electronics can be returned within **15 days** of delivery, not the general 30 days in RP-1.1. The day of delivery counts as day 0.

### CA-EL-1.2 Festive Season Electronics Window

For orders placed between 1 October and 31 December the electronics window extends to **30 days** from delivery. The general festive window of 45 days in RP-1.2 does **not** apply to electronics.

### CA-EL-1.3 Dead on Arrival

An electronics item that does not power on or is visibly broken when first opened is "dead on arrival" (DOA). A DOA item reported within **7 days** of delivery is replaced or refunded at the customer's choice, with free pickup and no restocking adjustment.

## CA-EL-2 Condition Rules

### CA-EL-2.1 Opened Anti-Tamper Seal

If the anti-tamper seal has been opened, the item can only be returned if it is defective (Return Policy RP-3.2). Change-of-mind returns on opened electronics are refused.

### CA-EL-2.2 Accessories and Packaging

All cables, adapters, manuals and the original box must be returned. A missing charger or cable may reduce the refund by up to 20% under Refund Policy RF-1.2.

## CA-EL-3 Batteries and Safety

### CA-EL-3.1 Swollen or Overheating Batteries

A power bank, charger or smartwatch that swells, overheats or smokes must not be shipped back by the customer. Stop using it, keep it away from flammable items, and contact support; the case is handled under escalation code **ESC-SAFETY** (Escalation SOP) and ShopSense arranges a hazmat-safe pickup.
