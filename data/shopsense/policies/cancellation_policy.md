# ShopSense Cancellation Policy

Effective 2026-01-01. Covers when an order can be cancelled and what happens to the payment.

## CN-1 Before Shipment

### CN-1.1 Processing Status

An order whose status is **processing** (not yet handed to a carrier) can be cancelled free of charge from "My Orders" or by contacting support. The full amount, including shipping, is refunded to the original payment method under the timings in Refund Policy RF-2.

### CN-1.2 Cancellation Refunds Are Not Returns

A cancellation before dispatch has no return label, no pickup fee and no warehouse QC step. It is not subject to the RF-3 approval tiers because the money was never captured as a completed sale; support confirms the cancellation and finance reverses the charge.

## CN-2 After Shipment

### CN-2.1 No Cancellation Once Shipped

Once an order has a shipment record with a tracking number, it **cannot be cancelled**. The customer may refuse delivery or, after delivery, return the item under the Return Policy and the category addendum for that product.

### CN-2.2 Address Changes

The delivery address can be changed only while the order is in processing. After shipment, the address cannot be changed.

## CN-3 Cancelled Orders

### CN-3.1 Status

An order with status **cancelled** is closed. It cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded again, because the payment was already reversed at cancellation.

### CN-3.2 Customer Disputes

If a customer says they cancelled but were still charged, treat it as a possible duplicate charge and follow Refund Policy RF-4.2.
